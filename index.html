EditorialDesigning a calendar cover

The Wildlife Calendar 2026 uses large numerals as its focal point. Narrow bands of wildlife imagery introduce variety while a shared amber palette holds the composition together.

PackagingReading a product label at a glance

The body wash kit cover pairs a prominent wordmark with fine botanical linework. The strongest contrast gives the name priority, and the illustration adds detail when seen up close.

Games & UIVisual cues that guide players

Contrast can separate an interactive object from its surroundings. A consistent shape or color can reinforce the same cue across environments, menus, and signage.
